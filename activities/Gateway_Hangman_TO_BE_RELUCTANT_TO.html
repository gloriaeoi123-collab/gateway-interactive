Clue: The man is being encouraged to try something, but he hesitates because he does not want to do it.

This expression means “to be unwilling or hesitant to do something”. It is followed by to + verb. The adjective begins with R.
Choose a letter, or use your keyboard.

Expression: TO BE RELUCTANT TO — to hesitate or be unwilling to do something.

“He was reluctant to try the trampoline, despite his friend’s encouragement.”

“The company was reluctant to admit that a mistake had been made.”

Language focus: be reluctant to + infinitive, not reluctant + -ing. Reluctance expresses hesitation or unwillingness; it does not necessarily mean an outright refusal.
