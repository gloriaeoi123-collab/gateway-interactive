Sentence 1: Selective Compression

HB · C1
Nominalise the event, action or process you want to make the “thing” the new sentence is organised around.

The aim is not to nominalise everything that can be nominalised. Choose what should be nominalised to make the sentence more compact and controlled without making it overwhelmingly complex.

There may be other parts of the sentence that could also be nominalised, but that does not mean they should be used in this particular rewrite.

Reusable strategy:
1. Find the main relationship in the sentence.
2. Identify the events that carry that relationship.
3. Nominalise those events.
4. Keep supporting or detail information in whatever form makes the sentence clearest.
The company admitted that it had misled customers, and investors immediately lost confidence in it.

1 · What SHOULD be nominalised here?

Choose the ideas that should become the main concepts around which the new sentence is organised.

2 · Nominalise the two chosen ideas

3 · Rebuild the complete sentence

Use those two nominalised concepts to make the sentence more compact and controlled. Keep the remaining information where it fits naturally.


Possible answer:
The company’s admission that it had misled customers caused an immediate loss of investor confidence.
