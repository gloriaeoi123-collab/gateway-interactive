From meaning to precise expression — interactive vocabulary practice

Your spidergram is your reference. Keep it beside you and consult it whenever you need to. Looking things up is part of learning. Work through Learn → Identify → Use → Make it yours at your own pace.
THINK · Your original vocabulary spidergram (click to hide or show)

Use the diagram to find expressions during the Learn stage. Hide it when you want to test your recall.

1 · Learn it

Find an expression in the spidergram that matches each meaning. Type it, check it, or reveal it. More than one expression may sometimes be acceptable.

2 · Identify it

Read the situations and identify the mental process. Choose an expression, then check your choice. Return to the spidergram as often as necessary.

3 · Use it

Choose the expression that makes each sentence more precise. Check the upgraded sentence and notice how the meaning is preserved.

4 · Make it yours

Use the spidergram to complete these ideas in your own words. There is no automatic marking: compare your responses with the examples, or discuss them with your teacher.

Possible responses — not the only correct answers

1. Before making an important decision, I usually ponder the long-term consequences.

2. People sometimes assume that a popular policy is necessarily an effective one.

3. If credible new evidence contradicted my view, I would reassess my position.

4. Governments should scrutinise the evidence behind expensive infrastructure projects.
