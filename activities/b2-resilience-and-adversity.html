Idiom: to weather the storm — to survive a period of difficulty.

“The company managed to weather the storm despite falling sales.”

“We have weathered the storm, but there is still a long way to go.”

Here, weather is a verb: weathered, weathering. The idiom emphasises surviving adversity, not necessarily resolving its cause.
