Vocabulary spidergram · Reactions and evaluations

Consult the expressions when learning; hide them when you want to test your memory.

really engaging
Admirationreally impressive

Enjoymentthoroughly enjoyable
surprisingly enjoyable
highly entertaining
Boredom / disappointmentpainfully boring
deeply disappointing
Strong disapprovalabsolutely appalling
absolutely disgusting
utterly ridiculous
Predictabilitycompletely predictable

Notice: collocations are not interchangeable. Deeply disappointing expresses a reaction to unmet expectations; painfully boring describes a lack of interest; completely predictable says the outcome was obvious.

03 · Use it

MB · B2 → Move from recognition to expressing your own reactions accurately.

These personal answers have form and completeness checks, not automatic judgements about your ideas. Compare your wording with the models and decide whether the collocation really fits your meaning.
5

Complete the reaction

Write the complete sentence, replacing the dots with a real example. Keep the highlighted collocation and, where necessary, add a reason.

6

Give your verdict

Write two evaluations of 40–60 words each. Use at least two different collocations in each evaluation. Explain why you reacted that way.

See a model evaluation

The documentary was absolutely fascinating. It explored a subject I knew very little about, and the interviews were really engaging. I was particularly impressed by the way the director presented different perspectives without telling the audience what to believe. I would certainly recommend it.

Final challenge: Read someone else's evaluation. Identify the expression that communicates interest, enjoyment, disappointment or disapproval, and explain whether it is appropriate. Accuracy matters more than exaggeration.
